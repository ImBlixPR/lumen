LUMEN - audiobooks with translated subtitles
============================================

Lumen plays an audiobook in the language you're learning and shows a translated
subtitle, in your own language, floating above every window. Everything runs on
your own computer.

What you need
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- About 3 GB of free disk space for the speech and translation models
- An internet connection the first time only, to download those models
- Optional: an NVIDIA graphics card makes it much faster. Without one it still
  works, using the processor.

How to start it
---------------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere, for example into Documents.
   Keep all the files together; Lumen.exe needs the "_internal" folder next to it.
2. Double-click Lumen.exe.
3. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't signed
   by a company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The first time, Lumen asks for the language of your audiobooks and the
   language you want subtitles in, then downloads the models (one time only).

How to use it
-------------
- Click "Add books" (or drop audio files onto the window). MP3, M4A, M4B and WAV
  all work.
- Click a book to play it. The window moves aside and the book floats at the
  right edge of the screen; move the mouse over it for the controls.
- Subtitles appear near the bottom of the screen while the audio plays.
- Shortcuts that work in any app:
    Ctrl+Alt+P      play / pause
    Ctrl+Alt+Left   back one sentence
    Ctrl+Alt+H      show / hide subtitles
    Ctrl+Alt+L      unlock the subtitles so you can drag them
- To quit: close the window, click "Quit Lumen" in the sidebar, or press Ctrl+Q.
  You can also right-click the tray icon (by the clock) and choose "Quit Lumen".
  To keep Lumen playing after you close the window, turn on Settings > Playback >
  "Keep running when the window is closed".

Good to know
------------
- For the best accuracy with an NVIDIA card, choose "Large v3 Turbo" for speech
  and "NLLB-200 1.3B" for translation (Settings > Models).
- Add the book's character names under the book title in Now Playing, written as
  they're spoken in the book (e.g. "Ernest, Célestine"), so names are spelled right.
- Your settings and library are stored in your user folder
  (%APPDATA%\Lumen and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lumen), not in this folder.
